## Summary

The convention that secrets (AIRTABLE_API_KEY, TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, LINEAR_API_KEY, SENTRY_DSN) are loaded from logs/runtime.env or the macOS Keychain, never from argv or command lines (to avoid ps leaks). docker-prune-safe deliberately avoids dot-sourcing runtime.env because its values contain |. jcode-mcp-config leaves secrets unexpanded in argv. This is a cross-cutting invariant across all external-access and notification scripts.
