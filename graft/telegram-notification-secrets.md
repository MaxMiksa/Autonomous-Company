## Summary

The shared notification and secret-loading convention: all watchers and brakes shell out to scripts/core/telegram-notify.sh, sourcing tokens from logs/runtime.env (or AIRTABLE_API_KEY env / macOS keychain). Secrets are never printed — verify-mcp-keys.py reports only lengths and shape status. This is the single escalation channel for operator alerts.
